Cruise v. Guillory Complaint

AI-enhanced description

This document is a complaint for declaratory relief filed by Cruise Automation, Inc. and Kyle Vogt against Jeremy Guillory. The complaint seeks to establish that Guillory does not have any equity interest in Cruise. It alleges that while Guillory had initial discussions with Vogt about potentially collaborating on Cruise in its early stages, the two decided not to pursue a collaboration. Guillory never received any stock, invested money, or contributed in any other way to Cruise. The complaint further details Cruise's operations and funding since inception without any involvement from Guillory. Declaratory relief is sought to remove uncertainty that could interfere with General Motor's planned acquisition of Cruise.
